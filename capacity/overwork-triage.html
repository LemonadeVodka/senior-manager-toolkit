Overwork Triage

When everything is urgent, nothing is. Overwork Triage takes 20 minutes and turns a wall of obligations into a short list, a few explicit trade-offs, and one message to your boss.

The Framework

  1. InventoryWrite down everything on your plate: projects, asks, recurring meetings, promises. Getting it out of your head lowers stress on its own.
  2. ClassifyMust (real deadline or real consequence) · Should (important, flexible timing) · Could (nice to have) · Drop (stop, or let it fail gracefully). For each, ask: who else could own this?
  3. Trade OffFor every new Must, pick one lever from the Trade-Off Menu.
  4. CommunicateTell your boss the trade-offs before you miss anything, using ARC.
  5. ProtectBlock time for the Musts, decline what you dropped, and re-run triage weekly.

The Trade-Off Menu

When something new arrives, at least one of these must change. Pick it deliberately.

Scope

Do less of it: an MVP, a smaller first version, or fewer features.

Time

Move the date, for this or for something else.

Quality

Lower the polish, consciously, where it's safe. Never on safety, security, or ethics.

People

Add or borrow people, or delegate it to someone who'll grow from it.

The Hidden Fifth Lever

"Me, working evenings" is the lever overworked managers pull by default. It's invisible to everyone else, so the trade-off never gets made. Use it rarely, and say so when you do.
