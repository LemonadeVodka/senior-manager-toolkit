Pillar 2 · Lead Your Capacity

Lead Your Capacity Playbook

Overwork isn't a character flaw, and working harder won't fix it. It's a system problem: more work than capacity, and nobody making the trade-offs visible. Your job as a leader is to make them visible, choose deliberately, and protect the time and energy that do your best work.

  • Triage, don't absorb. Inventory, classify, trade off, communicate, protect.
  • Every yes is a no to something else. Say which.
  • Push back with ARC: Acknowledge, Reveal the trade-off, Choose together.
  • Limit your own work in progress. Three big things a week, not twelve.

Weak

Says yes to everything, works nights, misses quietly, and burns out.

Good

Makes trade-offs explicit, pushes back with options, and protects focus time.

Best

Leaders bring trade-off questions to you, your team copies your boundaries, and capacity is planned, not hoped for.
