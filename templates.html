Template Library

Every template in the toolkit, ready to copy. Fill in the [brackets], cut what you don't need, and keep the tone calm and factual. Each one links back to the page that explains when to use it.

Lead Up

Upward SBI Feedback

Private feedback to your boss, tied to their goals. See Scripts.

ScriptUpward SBI
"Can I share an observation about [meeting/situation]?
In [situation], when [specific, observable behavior], the impact was [effect on people/outcome].
I know you want [their goal] — I do too.
Would you be open to [specific alternative] next time?"

Skip-Level Request

Build the relationship before you need it. See Skip-Level.

EmailSkip-Level
Subject: 30 minutes to share [team]'s progress?

Hi [Name],

I'd value 30 minutes to share what [team] has delivered this quarter — [one-line headline
result] — and get your perspective on [priority/challenge].

Would [two options] work? Happy to send a short pre-read.

Thanks, [Name]

HR Meeting Prep

Bring this, and send a summary afterwards. See Before You Go to HR.

NotesHR Prep
PURPOSE: [Advice / formal concern / request for change]

SUMMARY (2–3 sentences): [Pattern, timeframe, impact]

TIMELINE (facts only):
- [Date] — [what happened, who was present, exact words if known] — [impact]
- [Date] — [ ]

WHAT I'VE TRIED: [direct conversation on date, skip-level on date]
IMPACT: [on me, my team, the work]
WHAT I'M ASKING FOR: [ ]

MY QUESTIONS:
- How is confidentiality handled?
- What's the process and timeline?
- How am I protected from retaliation?
- What happens next, and when will I hear back?

Lead Your Capacity

ARC Pushback Email

Acknowledge, Reveal, Choose. See ARC Pushback.

EmailARC Pushback
Subject: [Request] — options

Hi [Name],

I understand [goal] matters because [their reason]. [Acknowledge]

To take this on by [date], we'd need to [slip X / reduce Y / skip Z]. [Reveal]

Options:
A. [Option] — [trade-off]
B. [Option] — [trade-off]
C. [Option] — [trade-off]

I recommend [B] because [reason]. Which would you prefer? [Choose]
If I don't hear otherwise by [day], I'll proceed with [B].

Trade-Off Message

Make the plan visible before anything slips. The Capacity Triage tool generates one for you.

MessageTrade-Off
Hi [Name] — I want us focused on what matters most. My plan:

Committed: [Must 1], [Must 2], [Must 3]
To make room: [item] → [reduce scope / move date / delegate to name]
Later: [items]
Recommend we pause: [items]

If you'd prioritize differently, tell me what should move. Otherwise I'll proceed from [date].

Boundary Email

Calm, specific, framed around doing your best work.

EmailBoundary
Hi [Name],

I'm committed to [goal], and I want to make sure I'm doing my best work on it.
Going forward, I'll [be offline after 7pm except for incidents / batch non-urgent requests
into our weekly sync / need priorities attached to new asks].

For anything genuinely urgent, [how to reach me]. Thanks for understanding.

Technical Pre-Read Request

Ask for the material that lets you ask good questions. See The Five Lenses.

MessagePre-Read Request
Hi [Name] — looking forward to [review]. Could you share a short pre-read covering:
- The problem and how we know it's real (data)
- Options considered and why this one
- Main risks, how we'd detect them, and the rollback
- Cost to build and to run
A page is plenty. Thanks — it'll help me ask better questions.

handover notes for [areas].

[Name]
