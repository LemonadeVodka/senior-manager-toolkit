Pillar 7 · Go on Offense

Go on Offense Playbook

The other pillars help you survive. This one helps you win. Organizations run on power: who's visible, who's trusted, who controls resources, who gets heard first. Robert Greene's The 48 Laws of Power catalogs how people gain and use it. Some of that is wise, and some of it is ruthless. This pillar takes the useful ideas, turns them into ethical offense, and teaches you to recognize the ruthless ones when they're aimed at you.

  • Know every move. Use the ones you'd defend in daylight. Counter the rest.
  • SPELL is your offense: Say less, Position, Earn leverage, Line up allies, Land on timing.
  • Counter-Spells are your defense against credit-taking, isolation, and undermining.
  • The Daylight Test is the line. Every move must pass it.

Playbook at a Glance

Use It WhenYou need a decision, visibility, resources, or protection for a project, or someone is playing politics against you.
The Plays
  1. Cast SPELL: build and use power deliberately
  2. Apply the laws in practice, the white-magic way
  3. Counter power plays aimed at you
  4. Run the Daylight Test on every move
  5. Plan your move with the Power Move Planner

It's Working WhenYour work is visible without you bragging, decisions go your way before the meeting, and power plays against you stop landing.
Weak

Believes good work speaks for itself, over-shares plans, fights in public, and gets outmaneuvered.

Good

Builds visibility and allies, chooses timing, says less, and documents.

Best

Quietly influential and trusted. Outmaneuvers without deceiving. People want you in the room.
