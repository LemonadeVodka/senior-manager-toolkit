The Daylight Test

Power without a line turns you into the boss you're trying to survive. Before you use any move from this pillar, run it through five questions. If it fails any of them, don't do it.

The Test

  1. DaylightWould I be comfortable if my team, my boss's boss, and HR saw exactly what I did and why?
  2. HonestDoes it involve lying, deception, or creating a false impression?
  3. HarmDoes it harm someone who doesn't deserve it, or damage their reputation unfairly?
  4. Legal and PolicyIs it within the law and company policy?
  5. TeamDoes it protect, or at least not hurt, my team and the work?

Passes and Fails

Passes
  • Pre-wiring a decision with allies
  • Timing an ask for after a big win
  • Building skip-level visibility through results
  • Saying less in a negotiation
  • Recapping decisions in writing
Fails
  • Spreading rumors about a rival
  • Hoarding information to make someone fail
  • Pretending to agree, then sabotaging
  • Taking credit for your team's work
  • Secretly recording colleagues

Why It Matters

Ethical power compounds. People trust you more over time, alliances deepen, and your reputation precedes you. Ruthless power works until it's discovered, and then it collapses, usually at the worst moment. You're playing a long game.
