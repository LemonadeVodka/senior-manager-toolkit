Pillar 1 · Lead Up

Interaction Debrief

After a hard interaction, spend five minutes writing it down while it's fresh. It calms your nervous system, shows you the real pattern over time, and gives you a factual record if you ever need one.

Private by design. Entries are saved only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded. Use a personal device, export regularly to somewhere private, and use Clear All on shared computers. Don't include company confidential information.

Log an Interaction

Turn It Into an HR-Ready Timeline

When you're ready to report, the HR-ready export turns this log into a clean, dated, printable timeline. Tag incidents as you go (name-calling, discriminatory remark, possible retaliation), so the pattern shows for itself.

How to Write It

Avoid

"He was a jerk again and totally humiliated me."

Write

"10:05, weekly review, 6 people present. Said 'this is amateur work' about my team's plan before we finished presenting. Two engineers stopped contributing. I said I'd follow up 1:1."

Facts, exact words where you remember them, who was there, impact, and what you did. Your feelings are valid. Note them separately for yourself. See Documentation Done Right.
