Owning Your 1:1s

With a difficult boss, an unstructured 1:1 becomes 30 minutes of reacting to whatever's on their mind. Own the agenda, and the 1:1 becomes where you get decisions, flag risks early, and build a written record.

The Structure (30 Minutes)

  1. Send the Agenda the Day BeforeThree items at most. Decisions first.
  2. Decisions Needed (10 min)Each with options and your recommendation.
  3. Risks and Early Warnings (5 min)No surprises. Bad news with a plan.
  4. Their Topics (10 min)Listen, clarify the priority, and confirm what "done" means.
  5. Wins (3 min)One team achievement, with names. It builds your record and protects your team.
  6. Recap (2 min, then in writing)"So we agreed…" in the room, then send the recap email within the hour.

If They Keep Cancelling

  • Send the agenda anyway, as an async update, with decision requests and defaults: "If I don't hear by Thursday, I'll go with option A."
  • Ask for 15 minutes instead of 30.
  • Note the pattern in your debrief log. Repeated unavailability, followed by blame, is worth recording.

Weak / Good / Best

Weak

No agenda. Their stream of consciousness. Decisions half-made and forgotten.

Good

An agenda sent the day before, decisions with options, and a recap sent the same day.

Best

They read your pre-read before you meet, decisions take minutes, and your recap thread is the record everyone trusts.
