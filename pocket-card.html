Start · Pocket Card

Core 5 Pocket Card

Five frameworks that cover most bad weeks. Print this page (it fits on one sheet), or save it to your phone's home screen.

1 · After a bad interaction

Reset

PauseWriteChoose

Send nothing for 20 minutes. Write the facts and exact words. Pick one move.

"Got it. I'll come back to you shortly."

 2 · Every incident and win

Documentation

Same DayFacts OnlyBack It Up

Date, setting, who was present, exact words, impact. Your own notes only, on a personal device.

"To confirm what we agreed today…"

 3 · Another "urgent" ask

ARC

AcknowledgeRevealChoose

Make the trade-off visible, offer options, recommend one, and confirm in writing. Two #1s means no #1.

"Doing this by Friday means X slips. A or B? I'd recommend B."

 4 · Technical debate

PROVE

ProblemRiskOptionsValueEvidence

Pick the two that matter most. Ask, don't assert.

"What's the most likely way this fails, and how would we know?"

 5 · The pattern won't stop

The Ladder

DirectSkip-LevelHREthics / Legal

Bring evidence at every rung. Skip the ladder for discriminatory remarks, name-calling, threats, or retaliation.

"Our team's delivery and retention are being affected by a pattern."
Before any counter-move

Daylight Test

Seen?True?Fair?Case-safe?

If HR read exactly what you did and why, would it help you? If not, don't do it.
