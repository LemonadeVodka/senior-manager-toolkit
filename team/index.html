Pillar 5 · Lead Your Team

Lead Your Team Playbook

When the pressure above you is high, your team feels it through you. Psychological safety, the shared belief that it's safe to speak up, ask questions, and admit mistakes, is what keeps a team learning and loyal. You can't control your boss, but you can create a pocket of safety beneath them.

  • Umbrella, not sponge: absorb the noise, pass on clarity, and don't soak up everything yourself.
  • Measure safety with a short, anonymous pulse every quarter.
  • Repair quickly after damaging interactions. Same day if you can.
  • Be loyal without lying. Never trash your boss, and never gaslight your team.

Playbook at a Glance

Use It WhenYour boss's behavior is reaching your team, people go quiet in meetings, or someone is being targeted.
The Plays
  1. Be an umbrella, not a sponge
  2. Run a team safety pulse
  3. Repair after damage
  4. Rebuild a team damaged over months
  5. Talk about your boss honestly, without trashing them
  6. Protect a targeted report
FrameworksAbsorb · Filter · Translate · Safety Pulse · Repair Conversation · Stabilize · Restore · Rebuild

It's Working WhenPeople still speak up and admit mistakes, attrition stays low, and your team describes your area as a safe place to work.
Weak

Passes the pressure straight down, vents about the boss, and nobody repairs anything.

Good

Filters and translates pressure, repairs quickly, and measures safety.

Best

The team stays steady and candid despite the turbulence above, and people want to join it.
