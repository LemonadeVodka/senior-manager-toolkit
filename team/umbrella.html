Pillar 5 · Lead Your Team

Umbrella, Not Sponge

An umbrella keeps the rain off the people underneath, and stays dry itself. A sponge soaks up everything until it's saturated. Your job is to shield your team from unnecessary noise without carrying all of it yourself, and without hiding the information they need.

Absorb · Filter · Translate

AAbsorbTake the heat, the tone, and the half-formed ideas. Don't forward the anger.
FFilterSeparate real priorities from noise and mood. Not every "urgent" is urgent.
TTranslatePass on the clear ask: what, why, by when. Calm, constructive, with context.
Forwarded

"The boss is furious about the incident numbers, and I got yelled at. We need to fix this NOW."

Translated

"Leadership wants incident numbers down this quarter. Let's pick the two biggest causes and plan fixes by Friday."

What Not to Hide

An umbrella isn't a blackout. Your team needs to know about real changes in priorities, risks to their jobs or projects, decisions that affect them, and feedback about their work. Shielding them from these isn't protection. It's a surprise waiting to happen.

Don't Become the Sponge

  • Have somewhere to put what you absorb: a peer, a mentor, a coach, a therapist.
  • Debrief bad interactions (Interaction Debrief), then put them down.
  • Watch your own warning signs.
  • Remember you're also allowed to push back upward (ARC).
