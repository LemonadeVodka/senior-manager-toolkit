Ground Rules

A few rules that keep this toolkit useful, fair, and safe for you.

What This Is

  • A practical playbook drawn from management research and experience, written in plain language.
  • Generic and fictional. The "difficult boss" in the examples is an archetype, not a real person.
  • A place to think clearly, rehearse, and prepare.

What This Isn't

Not Legal, Medical, or HR Advice

If you're dealing with harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or a health concern, talk to HR, an employment lawyer, your Employee Assistance Program, or a clinician. Laws differ by country and state, especially on recording conversations and on employment rights.

Privacy

  • The tools store data only in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Use a personal device for anything sensitive. Work devices may be monitored.
  • Never copy company confidential documents or data into personal notes.
  • Export what you want to keep, somewhere private. Clear the tools on shared computers.
  • Don't name real people if you share any of this.

Principles

Facts Over Feelings, Feelings Still Count

Write facts for the record, and acknowledge your feelings for your health. Both matter, in different places.

Assume Good Intent Until Patterns Say Otherwise

One bad day is a bad day. A repeated pattern is information.

Your Team Comes Before Your Grievance

Protect them first, and don't recruit them into your conflict.

Integrity Is Non-Negotiable

Even the Go on Offense pillar has a line. See the Daylight Test.

If You're in Crisis

If you feel unsafe or are thinking about harming yourself, contact local emergency services now. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, contact your local crisis line or emergency number.
