Pillar 3 · Lead the Room

OAR → DOA

Two bookends that make any meeting work. Open with OAR so everyone knows where you're rowing. Close with DOA, so nothing is "dead on arrival" after the meeting ends.

Open With OAR (2 Minutes)

OOutcome"By the end, we'll have decided X."
AAgendaTwo or three items, each with a timebox.
RRolesDecider, facilitator, note-taker, timekeeper.
ScriptOpening
"Thanks for coming. Our outcome today: decide whether we renew the monitoring vendor.
Agenda: 10 minutes on the data, 15 on options, 5 to decide.
[Name] is the decider, I'll facilitate, [name] is taking notes. Let's go."

Close With DOA (5 Minutes)

DDecisionsWhat did we decide? Read it back out loud.
OOwnersWho owns each decision and follow-up? Use names, not teams.
AActionsWhat happens next, by when?

Always stop five minutes early for DOA. A meeting that runs out of time without a DOA usually has to happen again.

The Written Recap

Send it within 24 hours (template): decisions, owners, actions with dates, and open questions. It prevents re-litigation, gives absent people context, and is your record if anyone later "remembers differently".
