Lead the Room Playbook

Meetings are where your calendar disappears, and where your credibility is won or lost. A few simple frameworks make the meetings you run sharper and shorter, help you decline the ones you shouldn't be in, and keep a difficult boss from derailing the room.

  • Purpose First: every meeting is Decide, Discuss, Inform, or Connect. Inform goes async.
  • OAR → DOA: open with Outcome, Agenda, Roles. Close with Decisions, Owners, Actions.
  • Recap in writing within 24 hours.
  • Audit your calendar and reclaim about 20%.

It's Working WhenYour meetings end early with clear decisions, you attend fewer meetings, and decisions don't get re-litigated.
Weak

No agenda, no decisions, the loudest voice wins, and nobody knows what was agreed.

Good

A clear purpose, OAR at the start, DOA at the end, and a written recap.

Best

People ask to join your meetings, because things get decided, and your recaps are the source of truth.
