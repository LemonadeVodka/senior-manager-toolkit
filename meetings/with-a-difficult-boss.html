Pillar 3 · Lead the Room

Meetings With a Difficult Boss

When your boss is in the room, the meeting can turn on their mood. You can't control that, but you can shape the conditions: what they see in advance, who presents, how you respond in the moment, and what gets written down afterwards.

Before

  • Send a pre-read 24 hours ahead, so their questions come early, in writing, not as a public grilling.
  • Pre-wire the key decision 1:1, so they aren't surprised in the room.
  • Prepare your presenters: likely questions, and permission to say "I'll follow up on that."
  • Plan your interventions: what you'll say if they derail or attack.

During

MomentLine
They interrogate a junior presenter"Good question. Let me take that one, and [name] and I will follow up with the details."
They go deep into the weeds"That's worth a deep dive. Can we take it offline so we can decide X today?"
They get heated"I hear the concern. Let's make sure we fix it. What would you need to see by Friday?"
They reverse an earlier decision"Last week we agreed X. If we're changing direction, let's confirm the new plan and what it replaces."
They dismiss an idea outright"Understood. What would make this viable from your perspective?"

After

  1. Recap Within the Hour"To confirm what we agreed…" (template).
  2. Check on Your PeopleA quick private word with anyone who took the heat (repair).
  3. Debrief YourselfIf it was a bad one, use the Reset Protocol and log it in the Interaction Debrief.
