Pillar 3 · Lead the Room

Running Meetings Well

Facilitation is a leadership skill. You're there to get to the outcome with everyone heard, not to talk the most. These techniques work in any room, including ones where a senior person tries to take over.

Techniques

ProblemTechniqueSounds like
Running longVisible timeboxes"We're at time on this item. Decide now, or park it with an owner?"
TangentsA parking lot"Important, and not for today. I'll park it and follow up."
Quiet voicesRound-robin, or write first"Two minutes to jot your view, then we'll hear from everyone, starting with [quiet person]."
One person dominatingAcknowledge and redirect"Thanks, that's clear. I'd like to hear from others before we decide."
Circular debateName the disagreement"We disagree on whether risk or speed matters more here. [Decider], which is it?"
No decisionDisagree and commit"We've heard the views. [Decider] will choose by Friday, and we all commit to it."

Remote and Hybrid

  • Put the agenda in the invite and on screen.
  • Use the chat for input from people who are less comfortable speaking.
  • Alternate meeting times across time zones, so the same people aren't always up early or late.
  • Call on remote people first. People in the room speak more by default.
