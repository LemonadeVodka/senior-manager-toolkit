Meeting Audit

Recurring meetings accumulate like sediment. Once a quarter, look at every recurring meeting on your calendar and decide what it deserves. The goal is to reclaim about 20% of your time.

The Audit

  1. List ThemEvery recurring meeting, with its length and frequency, and the hours per month it costs you.
  2. Ask Three QuestionsWhat's the purpose (Decide, Discuss, Inform, or Connect)? Would anything break if I stopped attending? Could someone on my team grow by going instead?
  3. Decide Each OneKeep · Shorten · Reduce frequency · Delegate · Make async · Cut.
  4. Tell People"I'm stepping back from this meeting to focus on X. [Name] will represent the team. Please tag me if you need me."
  5. Re-Check in a MonthDid anything actually break? Usually, nothing did.

Decision Guide

If the meeting…Then
Is mostly status updatesMake it async, with a written update and a thread
Needs your team but not youDelegate it as a growth opportunity
Is useful but longHalve it. Work expands to fill the time.
Is weekly but rarely has newsMake it fortnightly, or cancel when there's no agenda
Is a 1:1 with a report, or a skip-levelKeep it. Connection meetings are high value.
Nobody can explain the purpose ofCut it
