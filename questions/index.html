Lead With Questions Playbook

You don't need to be the most technical person in the room. You need to ask the question that exposes the risk nobody mentioned, recognize a weak answer, and translate the result for executives. Good questions earn more respect from engineers, and from a technical boss, than pretending to know.

  • PROVE: Problem, Risk, Options, Value, Evidence. It works on any technical proposal.
  • Curious, not interrogating. "Help me understand…" opens more doors than "Why didn't you…".
  • Listen for the weak answer, not the jargon.
  • Follow up. Credibility comes from remembering what you asked, and checking back.
