Question Builder

Pick the situation you're walking into and how deep you want to go. You'll get a short question set built on PROVE, with what to listen for. Copy it into your notes before the meeting.

Three Questions Are Enough

Don't fire off ten. Pick the two or three that matter most, ask them well, and listen. One sharp question asked calmly builds more credibility than a barrage.
