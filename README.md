# Senior Manager Toolkit

A personal playbook for senior managers working under pressure: a difficult boss, too much work, too many meetings, a technical room to hold, and a team to protect. It's organized into **seven "Lead" pillars**, one for each thing you're leading.

**Live site:** https://lemonadevodka.github.io/senior-manager-toolkit/

## Start With the Core 5

| Framework | Steps | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Reset** | Pause · Write · Choose | Straight after a bad interaction |
| **Documentation** | Same day · Facts only · Back it up | Every incident, decision, and win |
| **ARC** | Acknowledge · Reveal · Choose | Another "urgent" ask |
| **PROVE** | Problem · Risk · Options · Value · Evidence | Technical debates and design reviews |
| **Escalation Ladder** | Direct · Skip-level · HR · Ethics or legal | The pattern won't stop, or it crossed a line |

Every framework page opens with a **Framework Card**: Use When · The Steps · Say This · Do It This Week · Template. The [Start Here](how-to-use.html) page has a 15-minute path and a 30/60/90-day plan, and the [pocket card](pocket-card.html) prints the Core 5 on one sheet.

## The Seven Pillars

| # | Pillar | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead Up** (`up/`) | Difficult vs toxic vs abusive (a traffic-light spectrum and a Boss Pattern Check), the brilliant-bully field guide, **When It's HR-Reportable** (build the record, file, watch for retaliation, case-safe countering, and an HR-ready timeline export), the Influence Playbook, **Make Leadership Count** in a technical culture, scripts, 1:1s, the Escalation Ladder, and the Interaction Debrief |
| 2 | **Lead Your Capacity** (`capacity/`) | Overwork Triage (List · Rank · Trade) with a Capacity Triage tool, **ARC Pushback**, Force-Rank, the Weekly Big 3, and protecting your capacity |
| 3 | **Lead the Room** (`meetings/`) | Purpose First, **OAR → DOA**, running meetings well, a meeting audit, and meetings with a difficult boss |
| 4 | **Lead With Questions** (`questions/`) | **PROVE** (Problem, Risk, Options, Value, Evidence), a Question Builder, spotting weak answers, how to ask, and 30 core terms |
| 5 | **Lead Your Team** (`team/`) | Umbrella, not sponge; a team safety pulse with a tracker; repair after damage; **Rebuilding a Damaged Team** (a 90-day plan); talking about your boss; and when a report is targeted |
| 6 | **Lead Yourself** (`self/`) | Warning signs, the Reset Protocol, recovery and support, **Rebuilding Confidence** after sustained criticism, a Weekly Check-In, documentation, career insurance, Stay / Change / Leave, and exiting well |
| 7 | **Lead With Dark Magic** (`power/`) | **SPELL** (Say less, Position, Earn leverage, L1 Line up allies, L2 Land on timing), ideas from *The 48 Laws of Power* applied ethically, Counter-Spells, the **Daylight Test** (Seen? True? Fair? Case-safe?), and a Power Move Planner |

The Toolkit section adds a filterable template library (22 templates), the Core 5 pocket card, a framework index, a roadmap, and references.

## Privacy

- Every interactive tool stores data **only in your browser** (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent anywhere: no analytics, no network calls.
- **Back up** everything to one JSON file and restore it from [Start Here](how-to-use.html#your-data). Keep backups on a personal device.
- Use a personal device for sensitive notes, export what you want to keep, and clear the tools on shared computers.
- All examples are **fictional and generic**. No real people or organizations are described.

## Design

A Night Ops / Day Ops field theme: OD green, ranger green, coyote tan, field grey, and steel, with signal red reserved for danger and one muted tone per pillar. Barlow Condensed for headings, Barlow for body text and scripts, and JetBrains Mono for small labels. Text and background pairs meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes. Title Case headings. No build step or dependencies.

## Running Locally

Open `index.html`, or run `npx serve .`.
