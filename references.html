References

The ideas in this toolkit are summarized in my own words from these sources. Read the originals for depth.

Managing Up and Difficult Bosses

  • John J. Gabarro & John P. Kotter, "Managing Your Boss," Harvard Business Review (1980).
  • Robert I. Sutton, Good Boss, Bad Boss (2010) and The No Asshole Rule (2007).
  • Kerry Patterson et al., Crucial Conversations (2002).
  • Kim Scott, Radical Candor (2017).
  • Center for Creative Leadership, Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) feedback model.

Team Psychological Safety

  • Amy C. Edmondson, "Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams," Administrative Science Quarterly (1999).
  • Amy C. Edmondson, The Fearless Organization (2018).

Well-Being and Burnout

  • Christina Maslach & Michael P. Leiter, The Burnout Challenge (2022), and the Maslach Burnout Inventory research.
  • Sabine Sonnentag's research on recovery and psychological detachment from work.
  • World Health Organization, ICD-11 description of burnout as an occupational phenomenon.

Capacity, Focus, and Meetings

  • Greg McKeown, Essentialism (2014).
  • Cal Newport, Deep Work (2016).
  • Sophie Leroy, "Why Is It So Hard to Do My Work?" on attention residue, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes (2009).
  • Steven G. Rogelberg, The Surprising Science of Meetings (2019).

Power and Negotiation

  • Robert Greene, The 48 Laws of Power (1998). Paraphrased and interpreted ethically in Pillar 7.
  • Jeffrey Pfeffer, Power: Why Some People Have It and Others Don't (2010).
  • Roger Fisher & William Ury, Getting to Yes (1981), source of BATNA.
