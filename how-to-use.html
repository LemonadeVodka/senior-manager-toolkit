How to Use This Toolkit

You won't read this cover to cover, and you shouldn't. Use it at four moments: in the heat of the moment, in your weekly rhythm, before a hard conversation, and when you're making a bigger decision.

In the Moment

The two buttons in the header are for bad days. Bad Interaction? opens the Reset Protocol. Drowning? opens Overwork Triage. Both are designed to take less than ten minutes.

Every Week

Work through the This Week checklist: your Big 3, your boss 1:1 agenda and recap, a meeting cut, a team repair or recognition, your wins file, and the Friday check-in.

Before a Hard Conversation

Open Scripts for Hard Moments or the Template Library. For a technical discussion, use the Question Builder. For a high-stakes ask, use the Power Move Planner and run the Daylight Test.

Bigger Decisions

When you're weighing escalation or leaving, read The Escalation Ladder and Stay, Change, or Leave. Talk to someone you trust before you act.

Your Private Notes

Everything you type into the tools stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use a personal device rather than a work laptop for anything sensitive, export what you want to keep, and use Clear when you're done. See Ground Rules.
