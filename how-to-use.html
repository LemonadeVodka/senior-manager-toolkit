Start Here

You won't read this cover to cover, and you shouldn't. Spend 15 minutes on the Core 5, set up your private record, then follow the 30/60/90 plan. After that, use the toolkit at four moments: in the heat of the moment, in your weekly rhythm, before a hard conversation, and before a big decision.

The First 15 Minutes

  1. Read the Core 5 (8 Minutes)Reset, Documentation, ARC, PROVE, and the Escalation Ladder. Read only the Framework Card at the top of each page.
  2. Diagnose (3 Minutes)Run the Boss Pattern Check. If it lands in red, read When It's HR-Reportable next.
  3. Start Your Record (4 Minutes)Log the most recent incident in the Interaction Debrief, and save the pocket card to your phone.

The 30/60/90 Plan

Days 1–30: Protect
  1. Log every incident the same day, and back up weekly.
  2. Send a recap after every 1:1.
  3. Use ARC on every new ask, in writing.
  4. Stabilize your team (Rebuild, phase 1), and run a safety pulse baseline.
  5. If the conduct is reportable: read your company's policies, and book one consultation with an employment lawyer.
Days 31–60: Position
  1. Send your first Monthly Impact Note to your skip-level.
  2. Use PROVE in every design review.
  3. Line up two allies (SPELL: L1).
  4. Restore your team with visible wins and named credit (phase 2).
  5. Decide whether and when to file (Before You File).
Days 61–90: Decide
  1. Compare safety pulses: is the team recovering?
  2. Review your Weekly Check-In trend: are you?
  3. Escalate in order, or skip the ladder if it crossed a line.
  4. Make the Stay / Change / Leave call, with your career insurance up to date.

In the Moment

Use the two buttons in the header. Need a Reset? opens the Reset Protocol, and Drowning? opens Overwork Triage. Both take less than ten minutes. For anything else, the home page's Right Now table sends you to one page.

Every Week

Work through the This Week checklist: your Big 3, your boss 1:1 agenda and recap, a meeting cut, a team repair or recognition, your Debrief and wins file, and the Friday check-in.

Before a Hard Conversation

Open Scripts for Hard Moments or the Template Library. For a technical discussion, use the Question Builder. For a high-stakes move, use the Power Move Planner and run the Daylight Test.

Your Private Notes and Backup

Everything you type into the tools stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. That also means that if this browser's data is cleared, your notes are gone. Download a backup regularly, and keep it on a personal device or personal cloud storage, not a work laptop. Use Clear on shared computers. See Ground Rules.
