This Week

A small weekly rhythm that keeps you effective and protected. Ticks are saved in this browser only.

  • Monday: set your Weekly Big 3, and block focus time for them first.
  • Before your boss 1:1: send your agenda. Afterwards, send a written recap.
  • Every new ask: respond with ARC, not a reflexive yes.
  • Meetings: decline or delegate one meeting with no clear purpose (Purpose First).
  • Team: one repair or recognition conversation, with credit given by name (Rebuild).
  • Credibility: use PROVE in one technical discussion.
  • Protect yourself: log incidents the same day in the Debrief, update your wins file, and back up your notes.
  • Friday: do your Weekly Check-In. Be honest.

Last Friday of the month: send your Monthly Impact Note.

How This Toolkit Thinks

Patterns, Not People

Describe behaviors and their impact. That's fairer, safer, and far more persuasive.

Trade-Offs, Not Heroics

Overwork is solved by choosing, not by trying harder.

Umbrella, Not Sponge

Absorb the noise, pass on the clarity, and don't soak up everything yourself.

Options Create Calm

Documentation, allies, and a fallback plan are what let you stay steady.

Power, With a Line

Play offense deliberately, and keep everything you'd defend in daylight.

You Matter Too

A depleted leader can't protect anyone. Recovery is part of the job.
