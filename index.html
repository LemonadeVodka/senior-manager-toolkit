Do the Job, Manage Up, and Stay Whole

A playbook for senior managers working under pressure: a difficult boss, too much work, too many meetings, a technical room to hold, and a team to protect. It's organized into seven pillars, one for each thing you're leading. Every framework is short enough to use on a bad day.

The Seven Pillars

1 · Lead Up

Diagnose your boss, influence them, use scripts for hard moments, own your 1:1s, and know the escalation ladder.

2 · Lead Your Capacity

Overwork Triage, ARC Pushback, stopping multitasking, and protecting your time and energy.

3 · Lead the Room

Purpose First, OAR → DOA, running meetings well, a meeting audit, and meetings with a difficult boss.

4 · Lead With Questions

The Five Lenses for technical credibility without being technical, a Question Builder, and spotting weak answers.

5 · Lead Your Team

Psychological safety: umbrella not sponge, a safety pulse, repair after damage, and protecting targeted reports.

6 · Lead Yourself

Warning signs, the Reset Protocol, recovery, documentation, career insurance, and Stay / Change / Leave.

7 · Lead With Dark Magic

Go on the offense: SPELL, power ideas in practice, counter-spells, and the Daylight Test.

This Week

A small weekly rhythm that keeps you effective and protected. Ticks are saved in this browser only.

  • Monday: set your Weekly Big 3, and block focus time for them first.
  • Before your boss 1:1: send your agenda. Afterwards, send a written recap.
  • Every new ask: respond with ARC, not a reflexive yes.
  • Meetings: decline or delegate one meeting with no clear purpose (Purpose First).
  • Team: one repair or recognition conversation (Umbrella, Not Sponge).
  • Credibility: use the Five Lenses in one technical discussion.
  • Protect yourself: update your wins file and, if needed, your notes.
  • Friday: do your Weekly Check-In. Be honest.

How This Toolkit Thinks

Patterns, Not People

Describe behaviors and their impact. That's fairer, safer, and far more persuasive.

Trade-Offs, Not Heroics

Overwork is solved by choosing, not by trying harder.

Umbrella, Not Sponge

Absorb the noise, pass on the clarity, and don't soak up everything yourself.

Options Create Calm

Documentation, allies, and a fallback plan are what let you stay steady.

Power, With a Line

Play offense deliberately, and keep everything you'd defend in daylight.

You Matter Too

A depleted leader can't protect anyone. Recovery is part of the job.
