Pillar 6 · Lead Yourself

Stay, Change, or Leave

"I'm so tired of this" is a feeling, not a decision. This framework turns it into one. There are three real options, not two, and the middle one is often overlooked.

The Framework

SStayStay as you are, with better tactics, boundaries, and support. Right when the job still gives you enough, and the situation is workable.
CChangeChange something while staying: a different team, role, or scope, a new reporting line, a negotiated arrangement, or escalation that leads to change.
LLeaveLeave the company. Right when health, growth, or values are at real risk, and change isn't realistic.

Questions to Answer Honestly

  • Is my health holding up, or getting worse? (See your check-in trend.)
  • Am I still growing here?
  • Is the situation likely to change? Does my boss's boss know? Is a reorg coming? Is my boss likely to move?
  • How much do I value my team, mission, and compensation here?
  • What's my BATNA, and what's the job market like (BATNA)?
  • In a year, which choice would I be proudest of?

Decision Matrix

Rate each factor from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent) for your current situation, and set how much each one matters to you. Your answers stay in this browser.

Don't Decide on Your Worst Day

Make big decisions after a good night's sleep, and after talking to someone you trust. If you're in the red zone of burnout, get support first, then decide.
