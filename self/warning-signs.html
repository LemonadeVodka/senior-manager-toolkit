Warning Signs

Burnout rarely arrives all at once. It builds quietly while you tell yourself it's just a busy stretch. Christina Maslach's research describes burnout in three dimensions. Learn your early signs in each, so you act before you're depleted.

The Three Dimensions

Exhaustion

Tired even after rest, trouble sleeping, getting sick more often, dreading Monday on Saturday.

Cynicism

Detached, sarcastic, irritable with your team, "nothing I do matters," avoiding people.

Reduced Efficacy

Feeling ineffective, struggling to focus or decide, losing confidence in work you used to do well.

Traffic Lights

Strained

A hard stretch, but you recover on weekends. Act: protect your capacity and do the weekly check-in.

Depleting

Recovery isn't happening, and symptoms are spreading into home life. Act: cut load (Overwork Triage), take real time off, talk to someone.

Burned Out

Persistent exhaustion, hopelessness, health effects. Act: see a doctor or therapist, use your EAP, and consider leave. This is health, not weakness.

What a Toxic Boss Adds

  • Hypervigilance: constantly scanning for their mood, re-reading their messages, rehearsing conversations.
  • Rumination: replaying interactions at night.
  • Self-doubt: starting to believe the criticism.
  • Over-functioning: working harder to avoid criticism, which feeds the exhaustion.

These are normal responses to an abnormal situation. Name them, and use the Reset Protocol and the Weekly Check-In to interrupt the cycle.
