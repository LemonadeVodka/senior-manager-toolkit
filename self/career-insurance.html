Pillar 6 · Lead Yourself

Career Insurance

A difficult boss controls your reviews, your visibility, and often your next step. Career insurance takes back some of that control. With a strong record, allies, and options, you stay calmer and negotiate from strength, whether you stay or go.

The Five Policies

  1. A Wins FileEvery week, write down what you delivered, the impact, and any praise. It's ammunition for reviews and promotions, and an antidote to self-doubt (template).
  2. A Skip-Level RelationshipRegular, positive contact with your boss's boss, focused on outcomes, not complaints. If you ever escalate, they already know your work.
  3. Peer AlliesLeaders across the organization who've seen your work and would vouch for you. Help them first (allies).
  4. An External NetworkFormer colleagues, industry peers, and recruiters. Keep in touch while you don't need anything.
  5. Financial RunwaySavings that cover several months of expenses give you freedom to make decisions without panic.

Know Your BATNA

BATNA (Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement) is a term from negotiation research: what you'd do if this job ended. The stronger your BATNA, the more calmly you can push back, set boundaries, and escalate. Strengthen it continuously:

  • Resume and online profile updated in the last quarter.
  • Three people you could call about opportunities.
  • A clear idea of the roles you'd want next.
  • An internal transfer option explored, if your organization is large enough.
  • Savings runway known.
Options Change Your Posture

When you know you could leave, your boss's behavior loses some of its grip, and you'll often handle them better, simply because you're less afraid.
