Pillar 6 · Lead Yourself

Lead Yourself Playbook

You can't lead others well while running on empty, or while feeling trapped. This pillar covers both halves of taking care of yourself: your well-being (energy, health, support) and your options (documentation, reputation, and a real choice about whether to stay).

  • Know your warning signs before you're deep in burnout.
  • Reset in the moment. Don't reply angry.
  • Build a support system outside work: peers, mentors, professionals.
  • Options create calm. A wins file, allies, and a plan B make you steadier now.

Playbook at a Glance

  8. Decide: stay, change, or leave

It's Working WhenYou sleep better, you recover faster after bad days, your wins file is current, and whatever you decide feels like a choice, not an escape.
Weak

Grinding through, isolated, no record, no options, and burnout arriving quietly.

Good

Weekly check-ins, support outside work, a factual record, and an up-to-date resume.

Best

You're resilient, well supported, and choosing deliberately, whether that's staying and shaping things or leaving well.
