Pillar 6 · Lead Yourself

Recovery & Support

Sustained stress needs sustained recovery. Recovery means more than rest: it's real detachment from work, and people to lean on. Leaders who build both last. Leaders who don't, burn out, however strong they are.

Recovery Basics

Sleep

The first thing stress takes and the most important thing to protect. Keep a consistent wind-down, and no work messages in bed.

Movement

Regular exercise is one of the most reliable ways to reduce stress. A daily walk counts.

Detachment

Time where work genuinely isn't in your head: hobbies, family, nature. Research on recovery points to psychological detachment as key.

Real Time Off

Vacations where you don't check email. Plan them, and tell your team who covers.

Your Support System

WhoFor
A trusted peer (ideally outside your chain)A reality check: "Is this normal? Am I overreacting?"
A mentor or coachStrategy, perspective, and career advice
Friends and familyConnection, and life outside work. Don't make every conversation about your boss.
Employee Assistance Program (EAP)Free, confidential counseling at many employers. Often underused.
A therapistProcessing sustained stress, self-doubt, and anxiety. A sign of strength, not weakness.
A doctorSleep problems, physical symptoms, or burnout affecting your health
An employment lawyerIf you're facing harassment, discrimination, or retaliation
If You're in Crisis

If you feel unsafe or are thinking about harming yourself, contact local emergency services now. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, contact your local crisis line or emergency number. You don't have to carry this alone.
